Effector Sound

Collection Probes: Add your Custom Collection with Sound Probes.

Distribution: Choose the Distribution of each Probe Sound.

  • Iterate: The Probe ranges will affect the clones alternately based on their buffer numbers. Example: If 2 Probe ranges exist, clone #0 is affected by Probe range 1, #1 by Probe range 2, #3 by Probe range 1, and so on.
  • Distribute: Each Probe range will latch onto and affect a coherent part of the clones (with consecutive buffer numbers). For 2 Probe ranges, the first will affect the first half and the second the second half of the clones.
  • Blend: The different effects of the Probe ranges will be blended steplessly.

Use Curve Factor: Compatible with these BCloners: Grid Array, Linear, Radial, HoneyComb, and Spline. Uses the factor of the internal curves that use these BCloners.

Probe Sound

Sound Source: Add your custom Empty with Effector Sound Source Modifier.

Logarithmic Scale: This setting only affects the Amplitude graph's display. Normally, amplitude is displayed logarithmically so the lower frequencies, which can be heard best by the human ear, are given more room than the higher frequencies. The amplitude display (0%) and the logarithmic display (100%) can be blended steplessly.

Probe Strength: The amount of strength based on the audio loudness.

Channels: If you have a stereo sound file, this setting can be used to define if both or only one channel should be taken into consideration by the Effector. Mono channels are not affected by this setting.

  • Both: (Value: 0)
  • Left: (Value: 0)
  • Right: (Value: 0)

Low: Set the Low Cut of the Frequency Range.

High: Set the High Cut of the Frequency Range.

Bin Size: Adjust the Size of the Bin where the Field information is stored.

Time Offset: Offset the Sound Time position.

Time Range: Enables the Scene Time to move the Sound Position.

FFT Size: Number of samples to process in the discrete Fourier transformation at once. Higher values have higher frequency but lower time resolution, and vice versa.

Window Function: Applies a tapering function to the windowed samples to minimize discontinuities at the edges, improving frequency resolution and reducing artifacts.

Min: Adjust the Minimum value of Sound Amplitude.

Max: Adjust the Maximum value of Sound Amplitude.
